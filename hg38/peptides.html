Description

FragPipe (timsTOF diaPASEF) peptides at 1% FDR, projected to hg38 through the CDS of the RiboTIE ORF they were assigned to. Each item is one (ORF, peptide) pair, named ORF_id|PEPTIDE and labelled by peptide sequence; the amino acids are drawn from the genome (genomic codons).

Display conventions

Items are colored by PROSIT spectral-match confidence (brain-microprotein-atlas rubric: spectral angle x backbone fragment coverage), assigned for peptides unique to non-canonical ORFs: green = Strong, gold = Moderate, firebrick = Weak, grey = Insufficient or not graded.

Methods

Built by bin/make_peptide_gtf_file.py (peptideTrackUCSC, post_RiboTIE.nf) in the astrocyte-lrs pipeline; tiers from the NCORF_PROSIT subworkflow.

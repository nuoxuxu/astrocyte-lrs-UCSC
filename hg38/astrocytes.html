Description

Long-read (PacBio Iso-Seq) proteogenomic atlas of iPSC-derived astrocytes, unstimulated and stimulated.

  • transcriptome: ORFanage-predicted ORFs on SQANTI3-filtered Iso-Seq transcripts, plus non-coding transcripts.
  • translatome: ORFs supported by RiboTIE and collaborator Ribo-seq filtering.
  • riboseq: summed STAR unique-read Ribo-seq coverage across all samples.

Methods

Built by the UCSC_HUB subworkflow (post_RiboTIE.nf) of the astrocyte-lrs pipeline.
